## About the Author
Karan Bhardwaj is a Full-Stack Developer and Machine Learning Engineer based in Gurgaon, India.
He graduated with a B.Tech in Computer Science from Galgotias University, Greater Noida (Class of 2025).
Originally from Patna, Bihar, he has a background rooted in Physics and a deep passion for systems thinking.

His work sits at the intersection of:
- High-performance full-stack web development (Next.js, React, Node.js)
- Technical SEO and search engine visibility strategies
- Machine Learning and AI systems (PyTorch, LLMs, HuggingFace)
- 3D / creative web experiences (Three.js, React Three Fiber, GLSL)
- System design and scalable cloud architecture (AWS, Docker)
